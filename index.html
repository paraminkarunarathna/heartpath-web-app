Everything for your recovery, in one app

Made for the HomeTele-CR home cardiac rehabilitation programme.

  • Health Measures

    Record blood pressure, weight, sleep, steps and heart rate every day.

  • Exercise

    Exercises your doctor sets just for you, with timers and step-by-step guidance.

  • Medications

    Reminders at your medicine times. Mark each dose as taken.

  • Symptoms

    Record how you feel. Warning signs show call buttons straight away.

  • Mood & Relaxation

    Short mood checks, a breathing exercise and relaxing audio.

  • Education

    Easy lessons about your heart, food and medicines.

  • Charts & reports

    See your progress and share a PDF report with your doctor.

  • Emergency help

    One tap to call an ambulance (1990) or your family.

Questions

Is HeartPath free?

Yes. It is completely free and has no ads.

Which phones can use it?

Android phones with Android 7 or newer. It does not work on iPhone yet.

Is it safe to install from a website?

Yes. Phones show a warning for every app that does not come from the Play Store. HeartPath does not use the internet, and your information never leaves your phone.

Do I need internet?

Only to download it. After that, the app works without internet.

What if I change my phone?

In the app, go to Settings → Backup and restore and save a backup file (for example to Google Drive or WhatsApp). On your new phone, install HeartPath and restore the file.

How do I get updates?

Come back to this page and install the new version the same way. Do not uninstall the old app — your information is kept.

Does the app replace my doctor?

No. HeartPath supports your rehabilitation at home. It does not replace your doctor or emergency care. If you feel unwell, call your doctor, or 1990 for an ambulance.
